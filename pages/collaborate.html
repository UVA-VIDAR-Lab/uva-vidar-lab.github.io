Collaborate With Us


Who we work with

We study how people design, interpret, and communicate data visually and we're drawn to domains where those questions get hard. Our best collaborations happen when a domain expert brings deep knowledge of a real, unsolved problem, and we bring research expertise in visualization design, HCI, and evaluation.

If your work raises hard questions about how data should be communicated, designed, or how people may understand (or misunderstand) visual data, get in touch! We are always looking for new collaborations and are happy to discuss potential projects.
